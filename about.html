About Tiger Tech Consulting

Technology Made Simple, Reliable, and Built Around You

At Tiger Tech Consulting, we believe technology should work for you—seamlessly, reliably, and without frustration. Founded on a commitment to excellence and service, we provide high-quality audio/visual, home automation, networking, and IT solutions for residential and commercial clients alike.

Our leadership brings together a unique blend of expertise, discipline, and diverse perspective. One of our co-founders is a proud graduate of Tulane University in New Orleans and is a female minority entrepreneur who adds innovation, creativity, and a client-first approach to everything we do.
